02Market data, news and the Policy Tracker

Fuel Weather (including the summary strip on the home page), the trade press headlines on Market conditions and the Policy Tracker are updated automatically, directly from the third-party sources listed below, without manual review. Each refreshes every Monday. What you see is what those sources published at the time.

Fuel Weather sources

  • U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA): spot prices for conventional jet fuel (U.S. Gulf Coast), ultra-low sulfur diesel (New York Harbor) and natural gas (Henry Hub), averaged by month.
  • Ember: monthly average day-ahead wholesale electricity prices for Germany, used under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence.
  • Methanex: posted methanol contract prices for Europe, North America, Asia Pacific and China.

These are benchmarks chosen as signals for low-carbon fuel markets. They are not prices for eFuels, green methanol, SAF, renewable diesel or renewable natural gas themselves, and they are not quotes or offers.

Trade press headlines

  • Manifold Times: methanol and biofuel bunkering news feeds.
  • Offshore Energy: news feed.
  • Biofuels Digest: news feed.
  • U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA): Today in Energy.

Headlines are chosen by keyword matching, so some may be only loosely related to the fuel they are filed under. We show the headline, the publisher and the date, and link to the publisher’s own article. We do not copy, edit or endorse the articles, and the publishers are not affiliated with us.

Policy Tracker sources

  • U.S. Federal Register: rules, proposed rules and notices.
  • GOV.UK: UK government publications, under the Open Government Licence v3.0.
  • European Commission: transport and energy news pages.
  • International Maritime Organization (IMO): press briefings.
  • China’s National Development and Reform Commission (NDRC) and National Energy Administration (NEA): policy releases and notices.

Items are found by keyword matching, so some may be only loosely related. Headlines in other languages are machine-translated by DeepL and may be inaccurate. Summaries are ours. Always follow the link to the official source before relying on anything.
