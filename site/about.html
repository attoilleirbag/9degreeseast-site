About us

9 Degrees East

Commercial advisory for low-carbon fuels · Established 2018

9 Degrees East structures the long-term supply agreements that turn low-carbon fuel projects into financeable businesses.

Our experience comes from every side of the table: origination on an oil major's trading desk, commercial leadership at an eFuels developer, green methanol sourcing for a shipping company, and renewable fuels brokerage.

That range is the point. We build deals that work for producers and buyers alike, balancing price, volume, credit and sustainability terms so that projects reach final investment decision and the contracts behind them hold up over time.
