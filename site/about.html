About

9 Degrees East

We structure the long-term supply agreements that turn low-carbon fuel projects into financeable businesses.

We've done this work at an oil major's trading desk, an eFuels developer, a shipping company and a renewable fuels brokerage. The deals we build have to work for the producer and the buyer, on price, volume, credit and sustainability terms, so the project reaches final investment decision and the contract still holds years later.

9° East is the longitude halfway between London and Cape Town, the two cities Tom calls home. Halfway is also where most good deals get done.

Leadership

Tom Fox, Founder

A low-carbon fuels originator with 20 years structuring complex, multi-year supply agreements, since 2006, and 28 years in energy since his first role in Houston in 1998.

Tom has been an analyst, power marketer, oil major originator, consultant, broker, developer and buyer.

His career runs from power trading in the US and UK, through oil major biofuels origination, to eFuels and green methanol for shipping, across the Americas, Europe, Asia and Australia. He founded 9 Degrees East in 2018 and now leads it full time.

He builds deal structures that lenders will accept and that both producer and buyer can live with for ten years.
