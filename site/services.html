Ways to work with us

A second opinion

An hour or a day. Bring the term sheet, the price curve or the business plan, and we’ll tell you what a buyer, a seller and a lender will make of it. Day rate, no retainer.

In depth

Market assessments, offtake studies and commercial due diligence, written for people who sign things, with a senior lead accountable for the conclusions.

Part of your team

Tom joins two or three days a week as a fractional head of commercial or origination, or as interim cover while you hire. As long as it’s useful, and some of the best permanent hires start this way.

On the deal

Finding the right buyer or seller, then structuring and negotiating the commercial terms through to signature. We’ve done this from both sides of the table, which helps when working out what the other side actually wants.

Alongside your team

  • Commercial teams that need senior origination experience without a permanent hire.
  • Trading and origination desks building a low-carbon book.
  • Developers, lenders and investors who have commissioned a large study and want an independent read on its commercial assumptions. Plenty of reports are thorough; fewer are realistic.
