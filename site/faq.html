Common questions

Who do you work with?

Developers and producers of low-carbon fuels, the buyers of those fuels (shipping lines, airlines, fuel suppliers and industrial users) and the investors and lenders behind the projects. Tom has worked on each side, so we can advise any of them.

At what stage should a project get in touch?

The earlier the better, ideally before front-end engineering is complete. That is when the buyer strategy can still shape the project’s size, product and timeline. We also work with projects already past FID, on renegotiations, new contracts or regulatory changes.

Which fuels do you cover?

eFuels, green methanol, sustainable aviation fuel, renewable diesel and renewable natural gas, plus advanced biofuels and the inputs behind them: feedstocks, green hydrogen, CO2 and renewable power. See Products & Services.

Which markets and regions?

Europe, the Americas, Asia and Australia. That covers the regulation that drives demand in the EU, the UK and the US, and supply from regions such as China and Latin America. See Locations for where Tom has worked.

What is an offtake agreement, and why does it matter so much?

An offtake agreement is a contract in which a buyer commits to buy a share of a plant’s future output, at an agreed price or formula, for an agreed number of years. For a new low-carbon fuel plant it is usually the document lenders care about most, because it shows the plant’s revenue before it is built. Without it, most projects cannot raise debt.

What makes a deal “bankable”?

Broadly: enough of the output is contracted, for long enough, with a creditworthy buyer, at prices that cover costs and debt service with room to spare, and with risks such as volume shortfalls, regulation changes and certification clearly allocated. The exact tests depend on the lenders and the project. Our How we work page has examples of deals that closed and one that stalled.

Do you replace our lawyers?

No. We work on the commercial side: strategy, structure, pricing and negotiation. Your lawyers draft and advise on the legal agreements. We work alongside them, and many of the decisions in a long-form contract are commercial ones we can help make.
