Supply agreements

Long-term, bankable contracts that hold up over the life of the project.

01

What it is

The supply agreement is the long-form contract that carries the agreed deal for ten years or more.

It covers delivery and logistics, fuel specification, measurement, title and risk, invoicing, certificates and disputes. For lenders it is also security: they review it in due diligence and usually sign a direct agreement with the buyer.

  • Specification. ASTM D7566 for SAF blended into jet fuel, the IMPCA reference for methanol, EN 15940 for renewable diesel, and grid or vehicle standards for biomethane.
  • Certificates. How proofs of sustainability, mandate tickets and credits are issued, transferred and replaced if one fails.
  • Operations. Annual and monthly nominations, quality disputes, shortfalls and what happens when a cargo is off-spec.
02

When projects need it

  • After the term sheet is signed, in the run-up to financial close, when lenders and their advisers start reading every clause.
  • On the feedstock side too. Waste oils, biogenic CO2 and renewable power contracts are the other half of bankability, and a mismatch between feedstock and offtake terms is one of the most common reasons financing stalls.
  • During operations, at price reviews, annual nominations and when a rule change triggers a reopener.
