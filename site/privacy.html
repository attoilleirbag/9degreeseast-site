02What we collect

When you contact us

If you use the contact form or email us, we receive what you send: your name, email address, and anything else you choose to share, such as your organisation, project stage, the fuels involved and your message.

When you browse

Like any website, our hosting provider records basic technical information when pages are requested, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages visited, to deliver the site and keep it secure.

Our pages also load fonts from Google Fonts and code libraries from public code services (jsDelivr and cdnjs). To do this, your browser connects to those services, which receive your IP address.

What we do not do

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, we do not build profiles, and we do not sell personal data.

Some of these providers are based in, or process data in, the United States. Where personal data leaves the UK, we rely on the safeguards UK law provides, such as the UK–US data bridge for certified providers or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

06How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to respond and follow up, and for up to two years after our last contact, unless we go on to work together, in which case we keep records for as long as the law requires. Hosting logs are kept by our hosting provider for a limited period under its own policies.

07Your rights

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.
