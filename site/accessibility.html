01Our aim

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA, the widely recognised standard for accessible websites.

02What we do

  • Every page can be used with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline.
  • Pages use proper headings, labels and landmarks for screen readers.
  • Text and controls are designed for strong colour contrast on our dark background.
  • The site respects your device’s reduce motion setting: if it is on, animations are replaced by still versions.
  • Pages work at any zoom level and on phones, without sideways scrolling.
  • Information shown in animations, globes and charts is also available as text. Fuel Weather charts include a table of the numbers.
  • The contact form explains any problem next to the field it concerns.

03Known limitations

  • The 3D globes on the homepage and Locations page are decorative and cannot be explored with a screen reader. Everything they show is listed in the text beside them.
  • The animated Fuel Weather background is decorative; the fuel cards and their charts carry the information.
  • Policy Tracker headlines from non-English sources are machine-translated and may read awkwardly.
  • Links to other sites, such as official regulators or LinkedIn, are outside our control.

We are working to improve these.

04Tell us about a problem

If something on this site is hard to use, or you need information in another format, please email tomfox@9degreeseast.com and tell us the page and the problem. We will reply as soon as we can.

05About this statement

This statement was prepared on 20 October 2026 from our own review of the site. We will review it whenever the site changes significantly.
