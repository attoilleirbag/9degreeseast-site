Regulations

What RED, ReFuelEU, FuelEU, the RFS and LCFS mean for your contracts.

01

What it is

Regulation creates most of the demand for low-carbon fuels, and decides what each tonne is worth.

The same fuel can earn very different prices depending on which obligation it counts towards. We turn the rules into contract terms: which obligation the fuel meets, what that is worth to the buyer, and what happens if the rule changes.

  • ReFuelEU Aviation. At least 2% SAF at EU airports from 2025, rising to 6% in 2030 (including 1.2% synthetic fuels), 20% in 2035 and 70% in 2050.
  • UK SAF Mandate. 2% in 2025, 10% in 2030 and 22% in 2040, with a cap on HEFA fuels and a separate power-to-liquid obligation. Revenue certainty contracts under the Sustainable Aviation Fuel Act 2026 come on top.
  • FuelEU Maritime. The greenhouse gas intensity of energy used on ships calling at EU ports must fall 2% from 2025, 6% by 2030 and 80% by 2050, with e-fuels counted double until 2033.
  • RED III. Member states are bringing it into national law. It includes a combined 2030 target of 5.5% advanced biofuels and renewable fuels of non-biological origin in transport, at least 1% of which must be e-fuels.
  • US RFS and 45Z. Annual RFS volume obligations, and the 45Z credit, which runs to the end of 2029 and favours North American feedstocks. Latest volumes and proposals are on the Policy Tracker.
  • California LCFS. Amended rules took effect in July 2025 with steeper carbon intensity cuts.
  • IMO. Adoption of the Net-Zero Framework was postponed in October 2025. The Policy Tracker follows each round of negotiations.
02

When projects need it

  • Before choosing a market, to see where a given fuel earns the most once credits, penalties and certification costs are counted.
  • At contract stage, to agree who carries the risk of a rule changing, for example 45Z ending in 2029 or a review of an EU delegated act.
  • For e-fuels certification. EU rules on renewable hydrogen require additional renewable power and time matching, monthly until the end of 2029 and hourly from 2030, so power sourcing has to be designed into the project.
  • When a rule changes mid-contract and the parties have to decide what the change means for price and volume.
