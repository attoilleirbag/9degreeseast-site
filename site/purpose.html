Purpose, mission & values

Why we do this work, and the principles behind how we do it.

01

Our purpose

The world needs more low-carbon fuels. 9 Degrees East exists to help bring more of that supply online.

The technology for e-fuels, green methanol, SAF, renewable diesel and renewable natural gas largely exists. What holds most of it back is commercial: buyers, sellers and investors making sense of a young, fast-moving market and agreeing terms that work. That is where we help.

02

Our mission

To bring sound commercial judgement to the low-carbon fuel industry, whether that’s negotiating a contract or providing short-term commercial support to market participants.

A deal that only one side can live with rarely lasts. We aim for structures the producer can finance, the buyer can afford and the lender can rely on.

03

Our values

  • Both sides of the table. Tom has been the analyst, the originator, the broker, the developer and the buyer. We use that to find terms each side can actually sign.
  • Bankable, not just signable. A contract is only useful if a lender will lend against it. We test every structure against what financing will need.
  • Rigour. Deals are modelled, stress-tested and grounded in the regulation that drives demand, not in hope about where prices go.
  • Options over predictions. Nobody knows which market will pay best in ten years. We structure projects and contracts to keep more than one open.
  • Straight answers. If a deal will not finance, it is better to know early. We say so.
