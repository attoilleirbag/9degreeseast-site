Purpose, mission & values

Why we do this work, and the principles behind how we do it.

01

Our purpose

Low-carbon fuels only get built when someone signs to buy them. We exist to get those contracts written.

The technology for e-fuels, green methanol, SAF, renewable diesel and renewable natural gas largely exists. What holds most projects back is commercial: a buyer who will commit, at a price the plant can live on, for long enough to repay the debt. That is the gap we work in.

02

Our mission

To structure long-term supply agreements that take low-carbon fuel projects to final investment decision, and keep working for both sides once they do.

A deal that only one side can live with rarely survives ten years. We aim for structures the producer can finance, the buyer can afford and the lender can rely on.

03

Our values

  • Both sides of the table. Tom has been the analyst, the originator, the broker, the developer and the buyer. We use that to find terms each side can actually sign.
  • Bankable, not just signable. A contract is only useful if a lender will lend against it. We test every structure against what financing will need.
  • Rigour. Deals are modelled, stress-tested and grounded in the regulation that drives demand, not in hope about where prices go.
  • Straight answers. If a deal will not finance, it is better to know early. We say so.
