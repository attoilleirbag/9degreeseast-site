The route

Ten chapters, from a power analyst’s desk in Houston to the founding of 9 Degrees East. Select a chapter or keep scrolling.

1994–98 · Cambridge, Massachusetts

Harvard and MIT

Economics, BA cum laude

Studied at MIT, then Harvard, graduating in economics cum laude in 1998.

1998–2003 · Houston · Boston

Reliant Energy and Calpine

Analyst

Began in power: as an analyst at Reliant Energy in Houston, then at Calpine in Boston.

2003–06 · London · Toronto · Saltend, UK

Calpine

Commercial Manager, International

Ran the UK business of the 1,200 MW Saltend power station: its trading strategy and power sales, its gas purchase contract, raising project finance against the operating plant, and the deal team for its sale in 2006.

2006–13 · London

BP Integrated Supply and Trading

Commercial Analyst, then Biofuels Originator, Europe

Joined BP to help set up a new origination and finance group, then built its European biofuels book: long-term ethanol and biodiesel offtake with plants in development and in operation, structured with credit, securitisation and trade finance. In 2010, after the Gulf of Mexico incident, worked on crude oil financings of BP’s upstream assets in Angola and Azerbaijan.

2013–18 · London · Chicago

BP Integrated Supply and Trading

Global Biofuels Originator

Led origination and structuring for BP’s US and EU trading benches, across ethanol, biodiesel, advanced biofuels and methanol: second-generation technologies from lignocellulosic ethanol to HVO and gasification, feedstocks from corn and sugarcane to municipal waste, and pricing structures for bankable ten-year deals with new projects.

2018–21 · London · Europe · Africa

9 Degrees East is founded

Independent consultant in renewable energy

Incorporated 9 Degrees East Limited in September 2018 and began consulting that October, advising on deal negotiations, offtake pricing, risk management, project finance and commercial agreements, for clients in renewable diesel, RNG, LPG, ethanol and biodiesel feedstocks. Alongside, consulted with Channoil on renewable fuel markets across Europe and Africa, and wrote for Biofuels International on RED II, renewable diesel and the price effects of COVID-19.

2021–22 · London · Europe

OTX, an Xpansiv company

Head of Renewable Fuels

Led a three-person brokerage desk in physical biofuels and feedstocks across Europe, set its product and market strategy, and acted as Xpansiv’s internal renewable fuels lead alongside its carbon offset business.

2022–24 · Texas · Chile · Australia

HIF Global

Senior Vice President, Commercial

Led all global commercial activity for HIF’s eFuels developments, negotiating offtake for 1.4 million tonnes of e-methanol in the US, 20 million gallons of e-gasoline in Chile, where the Haru Oni plant in Punta Arenas produced its first e-gasoline, and future phases of e-SAF, while turning EU, US and Japanese regulation and the economics of hydrogen, CO2 and power into pricing.

2024–26 · London · Copenhagen · China

Maersk Tankers

Senior Green Fuels Origination Lead

Led supply origination of 800,000 tonnes of long-term green methanol volumes in China and structured sales into petrochemical and fuel markets, with pricing and volume terms flexible enough to carry several project FIDs at once.

Today · London

9 Degrees East, full time

Founder

Tom now runs 9 Degrees East full time, structuring the agreements that take low-carbon fuel projects to final investment decision.
